- Rice: https://download.mlcc.google.com/mledu-datasets/Rice_Cammeo_Osmancik.csv

- Titanic teaching copy: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data/master/titanic.csv (a public educational copy, not the user's Kaggle account files).
