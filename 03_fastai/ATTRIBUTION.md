# fast.ai source credits

The Part 1 course material is by Jeremy Howard and fast.ai. This folder includes
adapted exercises and local practice companions based on
[course22](https://github.com/fastai/course22) and the
[fastbook](https://github.com/fastai/fastbook) chapters. Each notebook retains its
source links and identifies the Colab or local changes. AI-assisted adaptations
do not change the authorship of the original teaching material.

The course22 source revision used here is
`230390584ba5b990e20487d68681a9ec5b258eff`. No separate `LICENSE` file was present
at that pinned revision. Material taken from fastbook retains
[LICENSE_FASTBOOK](LICENSE_FASTBOOK); do not treat the whole folder as having a
single replacement licence.

The source mappings and changes are recorded in
[the original-notebook provenance](../completion_criteria/fastai_originals_source_provenance.json)
and [the 00–07 provenance record](../completion_criteria/fastai_original_00_07_source_provenance.json).
